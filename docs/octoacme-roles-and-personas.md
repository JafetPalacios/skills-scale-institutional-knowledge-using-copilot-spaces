# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation across projects. They work closely with developers and product managers to define test approaches, ensure acceptance criteria are met, and identify risks before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality assurance approach for projects
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Execute and coordinate automated and manual testing efforts
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate with developers on test-driven development practices
- Validate release readiness and sign off on quality gates
- Mentor team members on testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure delivered features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Build team confidence in release quality
- Provide early visibility into quality risks and mitigation plans

### Typical Communication
- Test planning meetings with product and engineering leads
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Weekly QA status updates in standups and syncs
- Release readiness assessments and sign-offs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on test automation, test coverage, and defect triage
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria clarity and definition of done
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics, risks, and blockers affecting timelines
- **Technical Lead**: Discuss architectural testability and quality assurance approach

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads set the technical direction, make architecture decisions, and ensure code quality and maintainability. They mentor developers, identify technical risks, and bridge communication between engineering and product stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design standards for projects
- Review code and architectural designs for quality and adherence to standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Participate in estimating technical complexity and effort
- Coordinate technical integration points across teams
- Support troubleshooting of complex technical issues

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate team velocity through clear technical direction
- Build team technical capabilities through mentoring

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and technical guidance
- Technical risk escalations to project managers
- Participation in sprint planning and estimation
- Ad-hoc pairing and mentoring sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical direction, code review feedback, and mentoring
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Discuss architectural testability and integration testing approaches
- **Product Managers**: Translate product requirements into technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and dependency impacts on timelines

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads own user experience strategy, interaction design, and visual consistency. They collaborate with product managers and developers to ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX strategy and user research approach for projects
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Conduct usability testing and gather user feedback
- Ensure design consistency and accessibility compliance
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Document design decisions and maintain design systems
- Advocate for user needs throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and visually consistent user experiences
- Reduce support burden through improved usability
- Increase user satisfaction and adoption
- Build reusable design assets and patterns

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings with product and engineering
- Usability research findings and user feedback summaries
- Design specifications and interaction documentation
- Weekly UX status updates in project syncs
- Accessibility compliance reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on user research, feature requirements, and user stories
- **Developers**: Discuss design implementation, technical constraints, and feasibility
- **Project Managers**: Provide design timelines and review schedule impacts
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Review acceptance criteria for usability and accessibility

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and delivery teams. They gather and clarify requirements, document business processes, and ensure solutions address underlying business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather business requirements
- Document and clarify requirements in collaboration with product and technical teams
- Create business process documentation and flow diagrams
- Validate requirements against business objectives and success metrics
- Identify and communicate impacts of scope and priority changes
- Support UAT (User Acceptance Testing) planning and execution
- Translate business language to technical language and vice versa

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions address underlying business problems
- Reduce requirements misalignment and rework
- Increase stakeholder confidence in project outcomes
- Improve communication and transparency across business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions with stakeholders
- Requirements documentation and specification documents
- Impact analysis reports on scope and priority changes
- UAT coordination and execution
- Weekly business updates to stakeholders

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on requirements refinement and backlog prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Support impact analysis and timeline adjustments
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements and answer technical feasibility questions
- **Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder**: Provide business process insights and stakeholder feedback

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach teams on agile principles and practices. They enable team self-organization and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and escalate blockers affecting team progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and self-organization
- Track sprint metrics (velocity, burndown) and facilitate data-driven planning
- Support continuous improvement through retrospective action items
- Shield team from external distractions and scope creep
- Facilitate communication and collaboration within and across teams

### Goals
- Enable team to deliver consistent, predictable value
- Increase team velocity and reduce cycle time
- Build team cohesion and psychological safety
- Institutionalize agile practices and continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of daily standups, sprint ceremonies, and retrospectives
- Sprint metrics and velocity tracking
- Blocker/impediment logs and escalations
- Continuous improvement action item tracking
- Coaching conversations with team members

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on schedule impacts and blocker escalation
- **Developers**: Support self-organization and team dynamics
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and sprint planning collaboration
- **Technical Lead**: Coordinate on technical impediments and coaching needs
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Support quality metrics and definition of done enforcement

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are executive stakeholders who provide business context, strategic direction, and executive support for projects. They remove organizational barriers and ensure projects align with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context, strategy, and priority for projects
- Secure resources and resolve organizational blockers
- Make or escalate key business decisions affecting project scope or direction
- Review project outcomes against business success metrics
- Support change management and stakeholder communication
- Escalate issues affecting business impact or timelines
- Approve go/no-go decisions at major gates

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and executive support
- Enable teams to focus on delivery by removing organizational barriers
- Drive accountability for business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Executive decision meetings on scope and priority changes
- Business impact reviews at major gates
- Status escalations on critical risks or decisions
- Post-project outcome reviews and lessons learned

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive escalations on critical risks and decisions
- **Product Managers**: Align on business strategy and success metrics
- **Developers/Technical Lead**: Review technical trade-offs affecting business outcomes
- **Business Analyst**: Validate business requirements and stakeholder alignment

---

## Support/Operations

### Role Summary
Support/Operations teams own the operational aspects of delivered solutions, including customer support, monitoring, incident response, and production operations. They provide feedback loops from customers and production systems to the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Provide first-line customer support and troubleshooting
- Monitor production systems and detect operational issues
- Respond to and triage incidents following established runbooks
- Gather customer feedback and usage patterns
- Coordinate with development on urgent fixes and deployments
- Maintain operational documentation and runbooks
- Provide input on supportability and operational requirements during planning

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, observable solutions in production
- Minimize incident impact and resolution time
- Gather and communicate customer feedback and usage insights
- Reduce support burden through operational excellence

### Typical Communication
- Incident triage and escalation to development teams
- Customer feedback summaries and feature requests
- Production monitoring dashboards and alerts
- Post-incident retrospectives and action items
- Operational requirements and constraints during project planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Escalate production issues and provide customer feedback
- **Technical Lead**: Collaborate on observability, logging, and runbook creation
- **Project Managers**: Escalate operational risks and SLA impacts
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate on smoke testing and operational readiness

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When planning projects, ensure all relevant personas are represented and their responsibilities are clear.
- Use the interaction sections to understand cross-functional dependencies and communication needs.
